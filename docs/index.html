Every figure is decoded from raw Bitcoin bytes and describes what transactions request of the alkanes VM — an upper bound on outcome. Swaps revert on slippage or deadline; edicts clamp to available balance. Block ordering is approximated by ancestor fee rate, and private relays are invisible to a public mempool. Confirmed flow reports volume and direction only: historical pool reserves are not readable on the public gateway, so realized price impact is deliberately not shown.
